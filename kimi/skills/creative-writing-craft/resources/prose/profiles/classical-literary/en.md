# Classical-Literary Profile in English

Realize this profile within the history and present possibilities of English
prose. Choose the project's period, region, and narrative stance explicitly;
do not manufacture a universal old-fashioned English.

## Sentence Movement

Use coordination, subordination, periodic delay, and balanced clauses to let
thought develop across the sentence. Keep grammatical relations recoverable.
A longer movement should accumulate distinctions or pressure, not merely carry
more adjectives. Contrast it with plain statements when the judgment needs to
land.

Example: "He had forgiven the debt in public, where generosity cost him
nothing; in private, he remembered every penny." The balance exposes social
performance without an explanatory moral.

## Register and Narration

Let diction, address, and narrative commentary express class, intimacy,
education, and historical setting. Archaic words and spellings require project
evidence. A visible narrator may generalize, but should return the claim to a
particular act, object, or consequence.

## Allowed Variation and Failure Modes

Allow colloquial dialogue, concise action, dry comedy, and abrupt modern
clarity inside the broader register. Do not inflate ordinary observations,
overuse semicolons as costume, or imitate one canonical author mechanically.
Ornament that changes no perception is a failure, however polished it sounds.
