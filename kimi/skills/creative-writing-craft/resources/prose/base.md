# Universal Prose Base

Use these principles through the selected manuscript-language and prose-profile
resources. They describe reader effects, not one language's surface habits.

## Control Distance and Attention

Choose psychic distance deliberately. Move closer when the reader should share
a character's immediate perception or emotional pressure; pull back for
orientation, transition, and compression. In close narration, let vocabulary,
selection, and judgment belong to the viewpoint rather than announcing every
thought. Preserve the difference between what the narrator knows, what the
viewpoint character knows, and what the reader may infer.

Ground a scene with a few details selected by attention. A cook, musician, and
soldier notice different properties of the same room. A catalogue of every
sense is less immersive than one exact detail that reveals both place and
observer.

## Shape Movement

Let syntax, paragraphing, and density respond to pressure. Contrast creates
rhythm; no single sentence length or structure is universally vivid. A line
should be easy enough to follow for the intended reader, but clarity does not
require flattening strangeness, ambiguity, or a deliberately rough cadence.

Use scene and summary in proportion to significance. Dramatize moments the
reader should experience; summarize logistics, routine, and uneventful spans.
Showing everything exhausts attention, while telling every important change
denies the reader participation.

## Render Inner and Outer Life

Make interiority specific to this mind: associations, omissions, snap
judgments, memory, and attention. Reveal states through character-specific
choices or behavior when interpretation is part of the reward. Name a state
directly when the narrative needs efficient orientation instead.

Allow variation. Repetition, fragments, long movements, plain statements, and
ornament can all work when chosen for voice and pressure. The failure is
uniformity without purpose: the same distance, polish, and emotional altitude
regardless of what the scene is doing.

## Review Test

Ask what each passage changes in perception, pressure, knowledge, or feeling.
Judge surface correctness against the resolved language resource, register
against the profile, and matters of taste against evidenced author voice.
