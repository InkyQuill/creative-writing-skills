# Writing Staffing

Complete small, bounded tasks directly. Delegation is useful for independent
readings or separable workloads, not a prerequisite for good writing.
`$creative-writing-muse` owns coordination and its worker registry owns role
prompts and access. Do not maintain a second dispatch contract here.

Use the configured model unless the author or applicable host instructions
choose otherwise. Do not select a fixed number of critics by chapter importance.
More model responses do not automatically increase confidence.

## Choose a Purpose

- **Draft or revise:** one responsible writer for a passage, with relevant
  voice and continuity context. Split only genuinely separable outputs.
- **Review:** choose the requested focus or edit level. An independent review
  can help a consequential decision; do not require a write/critique loop for
  every passage or iterate until every model agrees.
- **Reader simulation:** use `$reader-sim` for a hypothesis about a possible
  reading. Filter secrets, desired effects, and prior critique before its first
  pass when feasible. Disclose existing exposure; do not claim actual reader data.
- **Explore:** separate useful angles when breadth helps. Let uncertainty and
  competing options coexist; no fixed number of perspectives is required.
- **Research or continuity:** assign a bounded question and the necessary
  sources. Independent lookups may run in parallel; dependent work waits for
  the facts it needs.

Use `$story-memory` for role-specific story context and `$creative-writing-craft`
for the shared prose-context procedure. Only file-writing tasks need assigned
output paths. A read-only role returns findings. Canon, accepted prose, and KB
updates remain under the caller's authorization and `$kb-management` procedure.

Synthesize against text and author intent. Agreement can suggest what to
investigate, not establish artistic merit or real audience response. When
working in the current context, do the task directly; do not simulate dispatch
forms or claim independent judgment.
