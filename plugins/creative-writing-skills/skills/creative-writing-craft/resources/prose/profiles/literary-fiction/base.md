# Literary-Fiction Prose Profile

Use this profile when literary-fiction tradition shapes the prose more than a
market-genre addendum would. It rewards exact perception, pressure of
consciousness, implication, pattern, and sentence-level necessity. External
events matter through the minds, relations, and social life they reveal.

Protect specificity of perception and thought. Let productive ambiguity open
meaning while keeping physical and causal orientation. Motifs, echoes, and
rhythms may accumulate pressure; contradictions need not resolve into a tidy
insight. Each sentence should change attention, relation, or understanding.

Allowed variation includes plain or lyrical diction, conventional or broken
form, comic distance, and strong plot. The profile does not require obscurity,
sadness, slowness, or prestige vocabulary.

Common failure modes are mistaking vagueness for depth, explaining a theme
after the scene demonstrates it, using lyric language that changes nothing,
and preserving ambiguity by hiding necessary information. Keep strangeness
when exact; cut ornamental fog.
