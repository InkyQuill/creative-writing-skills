# Light-Novel Prose Profile

Use this profile when the project wants the reading momentum, character
immediacy, and tonal elasticity associated with light novels. It is a prose
profile, not a claim about language, market category, age, or simplicity.

## Reader Effect

Keep the reader close to an active viewpoint. Make goals, reactions, social
pressure, and changes of emotional temperature easy to track while preserving
implication and surprise. Scenes tend to enter near the live beat and leave
with a changed question, relationship, or expectation. Dialogue, interiority,
and concrete reaction can carry exposition without pausing the scene.

## Register and Structure

Favor forward motion and readable paragraph shapes, then vary pace for impact.
Humor may interrupt danger; sincerity may arrive without ironic protection.
Recurring verbal motifs, controlled exaggeration, and sharp viewpoint
judgments can strengthen character presence. Give quiet consequences room
rather than treating every page as a cliffhanger.

## Allowed Variation

The profile permits lyrical description, complex syntax, slow chapters,
political detail, and mature themes when they deepen the intended experience.
It does not require first person, present tense, short sentences, or constant
banter. Apply the selected language adapter rather than importing another
language's published cadence.

## Failure Modes

Do not confuse accessibility with childishness, emotional clarity with
over-explanation, or momentum with breathless compression. Other failures are
identical character voices, exposition disguised as efficient dialogue,
reaction beats after every line, and tonal turns that erase rather than
complicate the preceding emotion.
