# Creative Writing Project

A shared model for distinguishing manuscript prose, provisional working material, and durable story knowledge.

## Language

**Manuscript**:
The prose that constitutes the story itself, including chapters and scenes at their current accepted state.
_Avoid_: Canon, knowledge base, work material

**Work material**:
Provisional material produced while planning, drafting, reviewing, or revising the manuscript; it does not become accepted prose or durable story knowledge without confirmation.
_Avoid_: Canon, manuscript, knowledge base

**Working draft**:
A provisional new chapter or revision that may replace or extend the manuscript after author confirmation.
_Avoid_: Accepted manuscript, canon

**Draft acceptance**:
The author-confirmed transition that makes a working draft part of the manuscript.
_Avoid_: Knowledge promotion, save, canonize

**Knowledge promotion**:
The separately confirmed transition that moves settled knowledge from provisional material or accepted prose into the story knowledge base.
_Avoid_: Draft acceptance, inference, autosave

**Story knowledge base**:
Durable story knowledge used across writing sessions, including established facts, synthesized references, terminology, continuity state, style references, and persistent issues.
_Avoid_: Draft, manuscript, notes

**Canon**:
Story knowledge the author has explicitly confirmed as established and safe to use as a constraint on future writing.
_Avoid_: Accepted prose, implication, working theory

**Contradiction**:
An unresolved disagreement between manuscript evidence and the story knowledge base, or between two durable story records; neither side silently overrides the other.
_Avoid_: Retcon, correction, stale draft

**Mechanical check**:
A deterministic assessment of whether project artifacts satisfy the shared project contract and their explicit cross-references agree.
_Avoid_: Literary review, semantic judgment

**Semantic review**:
An agent's evidence-based judgment about meaning, prose, and story consistency that cannot be established from the mechanical contract alone.
_Avoid_: Validation, schema check

**Project doctor**:
A project-health assessment that combines mechanical findings into prioritized, actionable repair guidance for an agent.
_Avoid_: CLI doctor, semantic review

**CLI doctor**:
An environment-health assessment that verifies the project CLI can be located and executed reliably by the agent.
_Avoid_: Project doctor, project validation

**Edit transaction**:
A logically atomic, journaled set of changes to project files that can be inspected and reversed without relying on version control.
_Avoid_: Git commit, autosave, synchronization
