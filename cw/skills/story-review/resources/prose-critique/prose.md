# Prose Review

Evaluate line-level rhythm, clarity, word choice, repetition, and the balance of
scene and summary. Resolve the manuscript-language, prose-profile, and
applicable project style/sample resources first. The selected prose stack
defines valid surface forms; this resource supplies transferable reader tests.

## Rhythm

Read at the intended pace. Ask whether syntax and paragraph movement respond
to pressure or repeat one template regardless of content. Variation is not a
quota: repetition may be deliberate, and different languages realize emphasis
through different structures. Compare against same-language, same-scope
evidence before naming a tic.

## Clarity

Can the reader follow action, reference, geography, time, and causality without
unwanted backtracking? Ambiguity of meaning can be productive; accidental
ambiguity about who acted, where an object is, or which event caused the next
usually spends attention on logistics. Diagnose the exact broken relation and
repair it through the selected language's syntax.

## Word Choice and Repetition

Judge whether a word is exact for this viewpoint and register. A broad word may
be right for speed or voice; a narrow word may add information or become
performative. Repetition is conspicuous in proportion to its distinctiveness,
placement, and function. Use the language resource and project baseline rather
than a universal list of weak, inflated, or machine-associated words.

Perception markers, attribution, dialect, and figurative language are choices,
not automatic faults. Investigate clusters that add distance, call attention
to themselves, or recur independently of scene pressure.

## Scene and Summary

Render important changes with enough concrete action, perception, dialogue, or
interiority for the reader to experience them. Summarize logistics and spans
whose texture is not the point. Naming an emotion can orient efficiently;
character-specific evidence lets the reader participate. Judge the intended
effect rather than enforcing a show-versus-tell slogan.

## Machine-Flavored Uniformity

Look for repeated syntactic templates, stock physical reactions, metaphor
families, explanatory endings, and the same polished emotional altitude across
unlike scenes. These are cluster signals, not proof of authorship. Anchor each
finding to reader cost and the applicable voice evidence. The useful revision
direction is more exact pressure, perception, and variation—not arbitrary
surface substitution.
