# Classical-Literary Prose Profile

Use this profile when a project deliberately draws on classical literary
traditions: sustained attention, social and moral observation, shaped
narratorial presence, and sentences whose form participates in thought. It is
not a license for antique decoration.

## Reader Effect

Invite deliberate reading without withholding basic orientation. Let scenes
accumulate meaning through perception, consequence, motif, irony, and the gap
between a character's account and the larger pattern. Reflection may occupy
real space when it changes judgment rather than merely restating events.

## Register and Structure

Permit a visible narrator, extended syntactic movement, periodic description,
and transitions across wider spans of time. Shape those resources around
attention: every clause should alter relation, emphasis, image, or inference.
Social register and forms of address carry history and power. Concrete life
keeps abstraction answerable to the world of the scene.

## Allowed Variation

The profile can be spare, comic, intimate, modernized, or polyphonic. It does
not require omniscience, long sentences, elevated diction, or historical
spelling. Select a language adapter and project evidence rather than blending
different national traditions into a generic "classic" voice.

## Failure Modes

Avoid ornamental fog, automatic archaism, essayistic commentary that freezes
the scene, and syntactic length without cumulative thought. Another failure is
museum prose: every sentence displays period flavor while characters cease to
want, misread, evade, and act.
