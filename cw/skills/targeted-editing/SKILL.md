# Targeted Editing

Use the smallest change that satisfies the request. A short revision does not
require an outline, full timeline, reader simulation, or project setup.
Use `/creative-writing-craft`'s shared prose-context procedure for language and
voice. Reuse context already resolved for this scope.

## Ordinary Pass

1. Identify the passage and the intended change from the current request.
2. Read current text immediately before editing, plus enough surrounding prose
   to understand voice and the joins. Inline text can be revised in the response.
3. Preserve working features and facts outside the change. Make the authorized
   edit, then re-read the passage and its joins.
4. Report the result and material limitations; do not require a placement report
   for a phrase change.

## Expand Context When the Change Warrants It

- For a line or phrase, inspect voice, meaning, and immediate context.
- For a beat, scene, bridge, reorder, or cut, inspect the affected setup/payoff
  and emotional or causal joins. Locate events on the timeline when order,
  duration, travel, or simultaneity changes; read only relevant character states.
- For changed facts, check downstream uses: knowledge, abilities, location,
  injuries, relationships, and world constraints. Distinguish claims from truth.
- For structural work, assess the intended form. Atmosphere, quiet, information,
  and companionship can earn space without escalation or a scene turn.

Do not enlarge the edit merely because a larger alternative is possible. If a
necessary change exceeds the requested scope, explain the mismatch and seek the
missing decision while continuing useful authorized work. Established canon
and terminology are constraints; propose retcons rather than silently applying them.

## File Operations and Durable Records

Use safe named edits for ordinary files, preserving concurrent and unrelated
changes. `/project-maintenance` owns exact-anchor operations, preview/apply,
and recovery when the managed CLI is applicable; load its operation guidance
only then. Never claim journal undo for an unjournaled edit. No scaffold is
required, and repairable tooling warnings do not block a readable literary task.

After editing, follow the specific ripples identified above. `/kb-management`
owns the shared promotion procedure after acceptance; `/story-memory` supplies
fact extraction and optional continuity formats. A prose edit does not grant
permission for unrelated record updates. Keep inferred implications and
unresolved conflicts provisional; report any dependency you could not verify.
